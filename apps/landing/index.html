The Onboarding-First Flow

Traditional SaaS

  • Heavy web dashboards
  • High drop-off on sign-up forms
  • Low email open rates
  • Friction before value

Messenger-Native

  • No apps to install, no passwords
  • Interactive chat onboarding
  • Instant 14-day trial activation
  • 80%+ open rates for notifications

Edge-first Architecture

  1. Supabase Edge Functions (Deno): Blazing fast webhook processing and business logic directly at the edge.
  2. Google Gemini API: Context-aware AI composition generating personalized "Cues" for users.
  3. Stripe Billing: Seamless transition from free trial to paid subscription via hosted checkout.
  4. PostHog & Sentry: Production-grade observability. Track funnel drop-offs and catch edge errors instantly.
