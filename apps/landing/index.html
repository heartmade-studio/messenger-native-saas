What “messenger-native” means

Traditional SaaS

  • Browser dashboard
  • App installs (optional)
  • Separate UX surface

Messenger-Native SaaS

  • Core UX in Telegram / WhatsApp
  • No new account paradigm
  • Billing via Stripe subscription

Case study: Moon Cue App

Moon Cue App supports partners by explaining what the cycle phase can mean in everyday moments and helping the couple communicate with empathy.

High-level flow

  1. Visitor lands on this page and starts checkout via the Stripe hosted link.
  2. Stripe confirms subscription on your backend (not included in this static landing template).
  3. Telegram bot uses your backend state (typically Supabase) to deliver reminders and guidance.
